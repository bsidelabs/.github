# B-Side Labs

**behavioral AI safety research lab**

Nearly a billion people interact with AI every week. The systems are scaling faster than our ability to understand them.

AI is increasingly influencing each other and us in ways we don't fully understand. We don't believe AI character will stay stable as systems grow more capable and get embedded in consequential systems.

B-Side Labs runs experiments on AI under pressure, studying the social and adversarial conditions that cause character to shift.

---

## What we do

We research the mechanisms of AI personality under pressure. Our work sits at the intersection of behavioral evaluation and AI safety, studying how and why AI character shifts under adversarial social conditions — and what that means for the systems being built on top of it.

---

## Why it matters

Current safety evaluation treats models as static artifacts. You evaluate the weights, check the outputs, ship. But models are social systems. They respond differently depending on who is pushing back, how, and with what claimed authority.

That behavioral variance scales with deployment. In a chatbot it produces biased answers the user cannot detect. In an agentic pipeline operating without human oversight, it becomes a safety failure — a model encountering an authority claim it cannot verify, shifting behavior, and no pre-deployment evaluation catching it.

Behavioral evaluation of adversarial social robustness is a missing prerequisite for safe agentic deployment. We build that layer.
